Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Cards, images, and study activity are stored on your device. There is no automatic upload to a developer server, account registration, advertising, or tracking.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Memotan (メモたん), its support website, and support inquiries, provided by hasumitomonobu.

2. Information stored on your device

Deck titles, questions, answers, notes, sources, pronunciation text, chapters, selected images, study progress and activity, and settings such as colors and speech preferences are stored on your device. They are not automatically sent to the developer. The app does not use your location, contacts, or microphone recording. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

3. Photos, files, and clipboard

The app imports only the images and study files you select. Photos are selected through the system picker. Clipboard text is read when you choose to paste. Sharing is initiated by you and sends the exported cards to your chosen app, AirDrop recipient, or file destination. Exported cards do not include study history. The destination service or recipient controls information after sharing.

4. External AI and speech

The app does not connect to an AI service to generate cards. AI prompts are prepared locally. If you paste a prompt or study material into ChatGPT or another external service, that service's terms and privacy policy apply. Check whether your material includes personal or confidential information before sharing it.

Speech uses Apple's speech synthesis features. Text is not sent to a developer server for speech. Apple services and device settings govern voice use and downloads.

5. Purchases and Apple services

Pro purchases and restores are handled through the App Store. The app uses purchase information verified by Apple to unlock features. The developer does not receive credit card numbers or other payment credentials. App Store and operating-system diagnostics are handled according to Apple's services and your settings. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Deletion and backups

You can delete cards and decks in the app's editing screens. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Offloading the app may keep its data. Delete exported or shared files separately at their destinations.

Memotan has no developer-operated cloud sync. Depending on your device settings, data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. Manage those backups through Apple's settings. The developer cannot remotely view, restore, or delete study content on your device.

7. Support inquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide. We use them as needed to respond or investigate problems, not for advertising or sale. Messages are kept in the email service and deleted when no longer needed for support or legal obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact us below.

8. Website and external links

This website uses GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process access information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. External sites have their own policies.

9. Changes and contact

Policy revisions and an updated date will be published on this page.
