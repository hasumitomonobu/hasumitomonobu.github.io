Help & Support

Turn what you want to remember into your own flashcards. Think of an answer, peek when you need to, and swipe through your learning.

Memotan is being prepared for release.

Try it free. Unlock more with one purchase.

The free plan includes 3 decks with up to 100 cards each. Practice, tests, images, speech, importing, and sharing are included.

Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. It removes the free deck and card limits and unlocks app colors and deck icons. The planned price in Japan is ¥300 including tax. Prices elsewhere are shown in your local currency on the App Store purchase screen. Imports remain limited to 2 MB and 2,000 cards per import.

Get started

  1. Your library starts empty. Choose Add Deck and give it a name.
  2. Open the deck, then Edit → Add Card. Enter a question and answer. Each side can have an image and its own text size.
  3. Choose Practice or Test, set your Study Range, and tap Start.

In Practice, answers are visible. Swipe left for the next card and right to go back. In Test, press and hold or drag to peek at the answer. Swipe left for Got it or right for Not yet. Bring the card back before lifting your finger to cancel. You assess your own recall; the app does not grade your answers automatically.

Import a set of cards

Choose Edit → Import Cards. Paste JSON, CSV, or TSV text, or select a file. Tap Review Cards to check and edit the cards before adding them.

Prepare an AI Prompt uses your deck title and any extra information to make a prompt you can copy. Paste it into an external AI app, then bring the resulting card text back to Memotan. Memotan does not generate cards using AI inside the app. Check the accuracy of all cards before adding them.

Share and save

Choose Edit → Share Cards to use AirDrop or save a file. Study history is not shared. Export important decks before changing devices. Memotan has no developer-operated cloud sync.

Restore a purchase

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for your purchase. Open Pro & Restore Purchases from the home settings menu, then restore. Purchases and restores require a connection.

Language

Memotan supports Japanese and English. The app uses your preferred supported language, with English as its fallback. Your own cards are never automatically translated. Speech language can be set separately in Speech & Autoplay.
